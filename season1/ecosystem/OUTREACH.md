# Founding Builders execution plan

## First cohort
Target a deliberately diverse small cohort across policy, guardrails, observability, agent frameworks and academic evaluation. Confirm maintainer contacts and interest before announcing participants. Avoid one-vendor dominance.

## Offer
An open integration specification, reproducible local harness, independently adjudicated evidence and an opt-in public result profile. Contributors can propose challenges and review public protocols; hidden variants and verdicts remain independent.

## Pilot design
One complete challenge, two independent systems under test, at least two governance configurations, paired baseline/protected trials, positive controls, reproducible local runs and an independent oracle. Include multilingual/speech variants when the challenge supports them; do not claim speech evaluation without actual audio tests.

## Milestones
1. Publish BYOG interface and neutral evidence protocol.
2. Validate a minimal adapter outside core and run architecture boundary checks locally.
3. Invite prospective builders privately with a reproducible demo.
4. Obtain consent, review licenses and conflicts, execute paired tests.
5. Publish a verified pilot with raw evidence and appeal process; only then enable badges and public rankings.

## Anti-capture rules
No pay-to-pass, privileged hidden variants, vendor-owned oracle, mandatory cloud service, misleading partner logos, or unverifiable marketing claims.
